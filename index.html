What students actually do

Hands-on robotics, engineering, programming, AI, competition, and leadership — not just lectures.

Engineering

Identify problems, prototype mechanisms, test, and iterate using the engineering design process.

Programming

Driver control, autonomous movement, sensors, computer vision, and intelligent systems.

Teamwork

Communication, leadership, documentation, presentations, and project management under real deadlines.

Featured projects

Students design, build, program, document, and present work that mixes robotics and AI.

AI Tic-Tac-Toe Robot

A robotic arm and vision system that reads the board, decides moves, and plays tic-tac-toe.

AI Object Detection

Camera + AI model that identifies common objects and announces them in real time.

FTC Competition Robot

From beginner to competition-ready

A clear path so new members know where they start and how they grow. Unit 1 materials are already live: FTC Mobility PDF.

  • 1. Foundations & safetyTools, teamwork, measurement, intro design challenges
  • 2. Mechanical designDrivetrains, gears, CAD, fabrication, iteration
  • 3. Electronics & sensorsMotors, wiring, power, troubleshooting
  • 4. Robot programmingTeleop, autonomous, debugging, telemetry
  • 5. AI & visionObject detection, cameras, responsible AI
  • 6. FTC readinessStrategy, notebooks, outreach, scouting, matches
