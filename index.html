Who We Are

A place for students to imagine, design, and build

Our robotics club welcomes students who are curious about technology, whether they are experienced builders or complete beginners. Members work together to solve problems, test ideas, and develop skills that can prepare them for college, careers, and future competitions.

Robotics

Design, build, program, test, and improve robotic systems through collaborative projects.

Artificial Intelligence

Explore computer vision, machine learning, object detection, and responsible uses of AI.

Engineering

Apply the engineering design process to real challenges using creativity, mathematics, and technology.
