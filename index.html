About FIRST Tech Challenge

HSHCS Trojans is FTC Team 26065. We follow the official FIRST Tech Challenge program from FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) — grades 7–12 students design and build robots for an annual game, with mentors, outreach, and STEM skill-building on and off the field.

61%of FIRST alumni declare engineering or CS by year 4 of college*

*Program stats from firstinspires.org/programs/ftc.

The program How FTC works: design and build dynamic robots for a new game each September, develop STEM skills, practice engineering innovation, and do community outreach. From beginner to competition-ready

A clear path so new members know where they start and how they grow. Unit 1 materials are already live: FTC Mobility PDF.

  • 1. Foundations & safetyTools, teamwork, measurement, intro design challenges
  • 2. Mechanical designDrivetrains, gears, CAD, fabrication, iteration
  • 3. Electronics & sensorsMotors, wiring, power, troubleshooting
  • 4. Robot programmingTeleop, autonomous, debugging, telemetry
  • 5. AI & visionObject detection, cameras, responsible AI
  • 6. FTC readinessStrategy, notebooks, outreach, scouting, matches
