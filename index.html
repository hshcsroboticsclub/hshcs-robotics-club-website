Our Team

HSHCS Trojans Robotics

The HSHCS Trojans are the official robotics team of the High School for Health Careers and Sciences. Our team welcomes students who are interested in building, programming, designing, competing, and learning how technology can solve real-world problems.

01

Engineering

Students apply the engineering design process to identify problems, create prototypes, test solutions, and improve their designs.

02

Programming

Team members develop programming skills for robot control, autonomous movement, sensors, computer vision, and intelligent systems.

03

Teamwork

Students build communication, leadership, documentation, presentation, and project-management skills through collaborative work.

FIRST Tech Challenge

FTC Team 26065

The HSHCS Trojans participate in FIRST Tech Challenge, an international robotics program in which students design, build, program, and operate robots for an annual competition challenge.

FTC gives students opportunities to develop technical knowledge while practicing teamwork, strategy, problem-solving, outreach, leadership, and professional communication.

Student Innovation

Featured robotics and AI projects

Our students learn by designing, building, programming, testing, documenting, and presenting projects that combine robotics, engineering, and artificial intelligence.

01

Robotics and Computer Vision

AI Tic-Tac-Toe Robot

A robotic arm and computer-vision system designed to identify game pieces, understand the game board, make decisions, and play tic-tac-toe.

02

Artificial Intelligence

AI Object Detection

An interactive system that uses a camera and an AI model to identify and announce common objects in real time.

03

Competition Robotics

FIRST Tech Challenge Robot

Students analyze the annual FTC game, develop a strategy, prototype mechanisms, program the robot, and prepare for competition.

Robotics Curriculum

A pathway from beginner to competition-ready

Our developing curriculum will connect classroom learning, robotics club activities, engineering projects, and FIRST Tech Challenge preparation.

01

Foundations and Safety

Lab safety, tools, teamwork, robotics systems, measurement, and introductory design challenges.

02

Mechanical Design

Structures, drivetrains, gears, mechanisms, prototyping, CAD, fabrication, and iterative design.

03

Electronics and Sensors

Motors, controllers, wiring, power systems, sensors, troubleshooting, and safe electrical practices.

04

Robot Programming

Driver-controlled programming, autonomous movement, logic, debugging, telemetry, and sensor integration.

05

AI and Computer Vision

Image recognition, object detection, camera systems, machine learning concepts, ethics, and responsible AI.

06

FTC Competition Readiness

Game analysis, strategy, engineering notebooks, outreach, presentations, testing, scouting, and match preparation.

Detailed curriculum maps, lessons, student resources, project guides, and competition materials will be added as the program develops.
