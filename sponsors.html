Sponsor the Trojans

FTC Team 26065 at the High School for Health Careers and Sciences builds robots, skills, and opportunity. Your support funds parts, tools, competition fees, and outreach.

Parts & tools

Motors, structure, sensors, hand tools, and fabrication supplies for the season.

Competition

Registration, practice fields, and travel so students can compete beyond the lab.

Outreach

Demo kits and materials for school events, younger students, and community visits.

Suggested support levels

  • Friend — $100+: listed on the sponsors page
  • Builder — $500+: logo + shout-out at demos
  • Season partner — $1,500+: logo on robot/banner (space permitting) + visit invite

Amounts are starting points — in-kind donations of parts, printing, or snacks help just as much. Final recognition details are confirmed with coaches.
