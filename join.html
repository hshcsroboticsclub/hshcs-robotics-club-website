Join the Trojans

For students and parents at the High School for Health Careers and Sciences. FTC Team 26065 — no prior robotics experience required.

For students

1. Email the coaches

Send your name, grade, and what you want to learn (building, coding, design, or outreach) to hshcsroboticsclub@gmail.com.

2. Visit a meeting

Meet the team, see the lab, and try a short hands-on activity. Meeting times are confirmed by email after you reach out.

3. Pick a starting role

Mechanical, programming, outreach, or documentation — you’ll grow into more as the season goes.

About FIRST Tech Challenge

Official program

FTC is a grades 7–12 STEM robotics program from FIRST. Students design and build robots for an annual competition game, do outreach, and build skills for school and careers. Learn more on the FIRST Tech Challenge site.

Scholarships & next steps

FIRST participants can explore scholarship opportunities through the FIRST Scholarship Program, plus career pathways on Classroom to Career.

For parents / guardians

What the team is

HSHCS Trojans is the school’s official FIRST Tech Challenge team. Students design, build, program, and compete with robots while building teamwork and leadership skills.

What to expect
  • After-school practices during the FTC season (schedule shared by coaches)
  • A mix of mechanical work, programming, documentation, and outreach
  • Competitions and community events as the calendar allows
How to get updates

Email hshcsroboticsclub@gmail.com with your student’s name and your preferred contact. Sponsors and visitors are welcome to ask about practice visits the same way.
